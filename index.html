# Privacy Policy — QBank Capsule **Effective date:** 25 October 2026 QBank Capsule (“the App”) is an educational application designed to provide question bank materials, PDF resources and other learning tools for students and teachers. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App. ## 1. Information We Handle Depending on the features you use, the App may handle the following information: * **Name:** Your name provided during registration or profile setup. * **Mobile number:** Your mobile number provided to the App. * **Academic session:** Your educational session or academic year, if provided. * **College name:** Your college or educational institution, if provided. * **Account information:** Information needed for account registration, login and authentication through Supabase. The App does not require a registration number as part of the information described in this policy. ## 2. Login and Authentication The App uses Supabase for authentication and account-related functionality. Information required for registration and login may be processed by Supabase to provide these services. Please do not share your password with anyone. ## 3. Photos and Image Selection The App provides an image-selection feature. When you choose to use it, you may select an image from your device for the relevant feature. Opening or using an image picker does not, by itself, mean that every image on your device is collected. Any storage or upload of a selected image depends on how the relevant feature operates. ## 4. Educational Materials and PDF Resources The App provides educational question bank materials and PDF resources. Technical requests may be processed to retrieve or display the resources you choose to access. The information handled during these requests depends on the specific feature and its technical implementation. ## 5. How Information Is Used Information handled by the App may be used, as applicable, to: * Create and manage user accounts. * Authenticate users and provide login functionality. * Maintain relevant academic profile information. * Provide educational resources and PDF materials. * Operate features requested by users. * Respond to support requests and address technical issues. ## 6.
